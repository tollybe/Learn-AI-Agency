# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they help learners become more independent, evidence-led, safe, and capable of teaching what they build.

Good contributions include:

- a project pathway with a real useful outcome;
- a better diagnostic or mentor question;
- accessibility improvements;
- examples of verification and debugging;
- privacy, security, or cost safeguards;
- translations that preserve the coaching intent.

Please avoid:

- enormous resource lists without a decision framework;
- streaks, points, or surveillance by default;
- claims of mastery based only on completion;
- hidden paid-service assumptions;
- real learner conversations or personal information without explicit permission.

Open an issue describing the learner need before a large change. In a pull request, explain the decision the contribution helps a learner make.
