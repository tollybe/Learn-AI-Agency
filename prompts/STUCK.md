# Prompt: I am stuck

Use this before asking a mentor to rescue the project.

```text
Help me debug this without taking over the whole task.

Goal:
[What should happen?]

Environment:
[Language/tool/version/OS—omit secrets]

What I tried:
[Commands, code, prompt, or steps]

What happened:
[Exact error or unexpected output]

What I expected:
[Expected result]

My current theory:
[Even if uncertain]

Please:
1. separate the observed facts from my assumptions;
2. give me the three most plausible causes in probability order;
3. propose the smallest test that distinguishes them;
4. do not rewrite the entire solution unless I ask;
5. after the fix, help me record what signal I missed and how I would diagnose it next time.
```
