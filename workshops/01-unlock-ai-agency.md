# Workshop: unlock your AI agency

**Length:** 75 minutes

**Audience:** people who have used AI or completed introductory training but do not yet feel able to build independently

**Outcome:** each participant leaves with a capability statement, a small project brief, a verified research decision, and a next build session

## Facilitator principle

Participants should use AI during the workshop. The lesson is not “AI is cheating.” The lesson is that useful AI work still requires framing, judgment, verification, and ownership.

## Agenda

### 0–10 min — The gap

Ask participants to privately write:

- something they can explain;
- something they have made work;
- something they can adapt without instructions;
- something they can teach.

Explain the evidence labels in the main README. A certificate can support “encountered” or “reproduced”; it does not automatically establish the later levels.

### 10–25 min — Replace the topic with a capability

Participants transform “I want to learn X” into:

> I want to become able to **[observable action]** for **[user/situation]**, so that **[outcome]**.

Pair-review the statements. A good statement makes it possible to imagine a demonstration.

### 25–40 min — Ask AI for options, not authority

Use the Agency Coach prompt. Ask for three small projects under real constraints. Participants must:

1. choose one option;
2. modify or reject at least one AI suggestion;
3. record why.

This creates the first evidence of judgment.

### 40–55 min — Research and cost gate

Choose one tool or platform decision. Ask AI to help compare options, then verify the important claims in current primary documentation.

Apply the cost gate:

- Is the named platform itself the learning goal?
- Who pays and what is the hard limit?
- Can a free/local route teach the same underlying skill?
- What requirement needs the paid/cloud route?

### 55–68 min — Define proof before building

Create an Agency Project issue containing:

- small useful outcome;
- normal, edge, and failure tests;
- privacy/safety boundary;
- artifact, reasoning, and teach-back proofs.

### 68–75 min — Commitment

Each participant schedules one build session and names the smallest first action. Do not assign catch-up work.

## Debrief questions

- Where did AI increase your range of options?
- Which decision still required your judgment?
- What would have made you accept a bad answer?
- What will your finished artifact prove that a course certificate would not?
